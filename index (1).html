About Cambridge English School

Cambridge English School was built to bring two things together in one classroom: fluent English and Sanatan values — so that every child can grow into a successful, healthy and happy life.

Our Vision

To cultivate English language and Sanatan values together in every child — teaching Yagya, Mantra, the Gita, the Hanuman Chalisa and the Vedas alongside the English our children need for the world ahead.

Our Mission

To build a community grounded in the spiritual values of Neem Karoli Baba, Hazrat Khan Baba, Swami Prabhupada and Sadhguru — combining modern education and the English language within a school that feels like home.

Four Pillars, One Crest

Our school crest carries four ideas we return to every day.

Wisdom

Learning that asks children to understand, not just recall — calm, focused classrooms built around real comprehension.

Discipline

Martial arts training that builds focus, resilience and self-control alongside academics, not instead of them.

Tradition

Yagya, Mantra, the Gita, the Hanuman Chalisa and the Vedas — keeping children connected to their roots as they grow.

Stillness

Time set aside for meditation, so children learn to be calm, centred and aware of themselves.

Founder & Principal

Deepak Arya

Deepak Arya founded Cambridge English School after 32 years in education. Over that time, he has taught English communication with a foundation in spirituality to thousands of students across Haldwani.

A black belt in martial arts, he has served on the executive committee of the Human Rights Association, affiliated with the United Nations, and has contributed to news channels and publications including Lead India News.

He has written three books, taught in medical and management colleges across the region:

  • Bhagwad Gita with Personality Development
  • Healing from the Essence of Hanuman Chalisa
  • Learn Spoken English

What We Teach

Play Group through Class 8, with English fluency and Sanatan values taught side by side, every day.

English & Communication

Spoken English is a daily focus, alongside reading, writing and the core CBSE-aligned subjects.

Sanatan Values

Yagya, Mantra, the Bhagavad Gita, the Hanuman Chalisa and the Vedas, woven into the week rather than set apart from it.

Martial Arts

Structured training built on the founder's own black-belt background, for focus and physical discipline.

Meditation

Short daily practice so children build the habit of stillness early.
