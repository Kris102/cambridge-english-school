About Us

Cambridge English School was built to bring two things together in one classroom: fluent English and Sanatan values — so that every child can grow into a successful, healthy and happy life.

Our Vision

"Cambridge English School" is built with the intention of developing and inculcating Sanatan values and the English language in children, so that they can go on to lead a successful, healthy and happy life.

Our school works to develop English language skills while cultivating Hindu Sanatan values, teaching Yoga, Mantra, the Gita, the Hanuman Chalisa, the Vedas and more.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a model society rooted in the spiritual values of "Vishwam Kalyan", "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam", "Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinah" and "Sarve Santu Niramayah" — and to establish a school that functions like a commune, in the spirit of the Isha Foundation, Dol Ashram or ISKCON, combining modern education with the English language.

Four Pillars, One Crest

Our school crest carries four ideas we return to every day.

Wisdom

Learning that asks children to understand, not just recall — calm, focused classrooms built around real comprehension.

Discipline

Martial arts training that builds focus, resilience and self-control alongside academics, not instead of them.

Tradition

Yagya, Mantra, the Gita, the Hanuman Chalisa and the Vedas — keeping children connected to their roots as they grow.

Stillness

Time set aside for meditation, so children learn to be calm, centred and aware of themselves.

About Principal

The Founder — Deepak Arya

Deepak Arya, founder of Cambridge English School and Cambridge Airhostess Academy, has been in the field of education for the past 32 years.

He has taught more than ten thousand students, including English communication and personality development.

A Black Belt in Martial Arts, he has also worked for society as an ex-member of the Human Rights Association, affiliated with the United Nations, and has contributed to various news channels and newspapers, including Lead India News.

Deepak Arya has written several significant books, which he has promoted across medical colleges, management colleges and other institutions:

  • Personality Development Skills
  • Bhagavad Gita with Personality Development
  • Healing from the Essence of Hanuman Chalisa
  • Learn Spoken English

Important Fee Information

  • One-time Admission Fee: A one-time, non-refundable admission fee applies at the time of admission.
  • Monthly Fee System: Fees are collected monthly, not on a quarterly basis.
  • Fee Includes: Tuition, activities, English communication, personality development, summer air-conditioned classes, and other school facilities.
  • Vacations: Summer, winter and other declared holidays are part of the school's academic calendar. An additional ₹100 applies during the month of unit tests and examinations.
  • Transport: The school does not provide a transport facility.
